# Dedication

For Satoshi Nakamoto, who stepped out of the light so that no one would need to be trusted, and showed that power shared by everyone belongs to no one.

The genesis block holds 50 EST locked forever, spendable by no one: a marker, not a fortune.

# Mission

- **Free trade:** anyone, anywhere, should be able to offer and ask for goods and services, peer to peer,
  with **no third party** in the deal. Any two pseudonymous parties trade directly; neither learns the other's
  real identity, and no platform sits in the middle. That has to stay within the law (see TERMS.md).
- **Spreading intelligence:** local AI agents could one day help people find matches, describe items and spot
  fraud. They would run on users' own devices and use only public listings.
- **Mars:** a marketplace that needs no central server, tolerates delay and syncs by gossip is the kind of
  tool a far-away, intermittently connected settlement would need. We design for partitions and late sync.
